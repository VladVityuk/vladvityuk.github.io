# Privacy Policy and Data Deletion: chomu-studio

**App:** chomu-studio (Meta App ID 1455612306530192)
**Developer:** Vlad Vityuk
**Contact:** vlad.vityuk.apps@gmail.com
**Effective date:** 2026-11-05

## What the app is

chomu-studio is a private publishing tool used only by its developer to publish content to his own Instagram account @chomu.my.kupuyemo and his own Facebook Page «Чому ми купуємо». It is not offered to other users, and nobody else can log in to it.

## Information the app handles

- Access tokens for the developer's own Instagram account and Facebook Page, stored only on the developer's own computer.
- Insights (statistics) of the developer's own posts and account.
- Comments left by other people on the developer's own posts: the username, comment text and time, as returned by the Instagram API. They are used only to read and reply to those comments.

The app does not collect any other personal data, does not use cookies or tracking, does not sell or share data with third parties, and does not use the data for advertising.

## Storage and security

All data is stored locally on the developer's computer. There is no public server or database. Published media files are uploaded temporarily to a private storage bucket only so that Instagram and Facebook can fetch them.

## Data deletion

If you commented on @chomu.my.kupuyemo or on the Facebook Page «Чому ми купуємо» and want your data removed from this app, send an email to **vlad.vityuk.apps@gmail.com** with your username and the post. Your comment data will be deleted from the app within 30 days and you will get a confirmation by email. Deleting your comment on Instagram or Facebook also removes it from the app on the next sync.

## Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with a new effective date.
